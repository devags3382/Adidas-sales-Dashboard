## Project Overview

In today's competitive retail industry, understanding sales performance, customer preferences, product demand, and regional trends is essential for business growth. This project analyzes Adidas sales data to identify key performance indicators, sales patterns, customer purchasing behavior, and profitability drivers.

The dashboard provides actionable insights that help optimize product strategy, improve sales performance, increase profitability, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

# Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyze the Adidas sales database and identify key insights that can improve sales performance and optimize business strategies.

By examining sales transactions across retailers, regions, product categories, genders, and sales channels, the project aims to uncover trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and identify opportunities for business growth.

---

# Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
* Identify top-performing retailers and cities.
* Understand product category sales distribution.
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior by gender.
* Analyze sales trends across different time periods.
* Compare sales performance across regions, states, and cities.
* Assess the effectiveness of various sales channels.
* Provide recommendations to increase revenue and profitability.

# Key Insights

## Overall Business Performance

* Adidas generated approximately **$120 Million in Total Sales**.
* Total Operating Profit reached approximately **$47 Million**.
* The business demonstrates strong profitability and market demand.

---

## Top Performing Cities

The highest profit-generating cities are:

1. Charleston
2. New York

These cities contribute significantly to Adidas' overall profitability.

---

## Sales Trend Analysis

* Sales steadily increase from **Quarter 1 (Q1) through Quarter 3 (Q3)**.
* A slight decline in sales is observed during **Quarter 4 (Q4)**.

This suggests potential seasonal demand fluctuations toward the end of the year.

---

## Retailer Performance

* **West Gear** is the most profitable retailer in the Adidas network.
* The retailer consistently generates higher profits compared to other partners.

---

## Sales Channel Performance

Customers show a stronger preference for:

* Outlet Stores
* In-Store Purchases

Online sales contribute less compared to offline channels.

---

## Gender-Based Product Preferences

### Male Customers

Most frequently purchase:

* Street Footwear
* Athletic Footwear

### Female Customers

Most frequently purchase:

* Apparel Products

This indicates clear differences in purchasing behavior across customer segments.

---

# Recommendations

## 1. Increase Production of Street Footwear

Since Street Footwear is one of the most demanded product categories:

* Increase inventory availability.
* Expand product variations.
* Launch new designs and collections.

This can help maximize revenue and meet customer demand.

---

## 2. Strengthen Online Sales Strategy

To improve online sales performance:

* Offer exclusive online discounts.
* Invest in digital marketing campaigns.
* Improve website and mobile shopping experience.
* Introduce personalized product recommendations.

---

## 3. Launch Seasonal Promotions During Q4

Since sales decline in Quarter 4:

* Introduce seasonal sales campaigns.
* Offer holiday discounts and bundle deals.
* Create limited-edition product launches.

These strategies can help offset seasonal slowdowns.

---

## 4. Expand Marketplace Presence

Increase product availability on major e-commerce platforms such as:

* Amazon
* Walmart

This can improve brand visibility and attract additional customers.

---

## 5. Expand In-Store Product Categories

Since customers prefer offline shopping:

* Add more product categories in physical stores.
* Improve product displays and in-store experiences.
* Introduce exclusive store-only collections.

---

## 6. Leverage High-Performing Cities

Focus marketing investments in:

* Charleston
* New York

Replicate successful sales strategies from these markets in other regions.
